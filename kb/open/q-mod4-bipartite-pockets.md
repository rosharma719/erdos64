# G1′: mod-4 rigidity in bipartite cores whose pockets all have ≥ 3 attachments

## Question

In a 2-connected bounded-degree bipartite α-expander with δ ≥ 3, show that among many disjoint candidate cycles, some yield a gadget with step ≡ 2 (mod 4), in the configuration where giant-attached vertices are pairwise non-adjacent and every clean pocket has ≥ 3 attachments.

## Context

It is the only remaining genuinely mathematical gap in the expander pillar.
