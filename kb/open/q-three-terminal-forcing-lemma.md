# Three-terminal path-forcing lemma (equivalent to EG)

## Question

Let H be a 2-degenerate graph with terminals a, b, c, every non-terminal of degree ≥ 3, every terminal of degree ≥ 2, and no cycle of length 2^k. Show that some pair of terminals is joined by a simple path of length 2^k − 2 for some k ≥ 2.

## Evidence

- `kb/runs/2026-11-17-handoff/handoff/Erdos_Gyarfas_Full_Research_Dossier.md#7`

## Caveats

Equivalent to Erdős–Gyárfás (short proof, dossier §7.2), so it is a reformulation, not an easier target. Deleting any vertex of a connected cubic graph already gives a 2-degenerate graph, so it contains the whole cubic difficulty (§7.3).
