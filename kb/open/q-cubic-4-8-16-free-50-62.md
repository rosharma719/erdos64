# Finite band: no {C4,C8,C16}-free cubic graph on 50–62 vertices?

## Question

Decide whether a cubic graph on 50–62 vertices avoids C4, C8 and C16. A negative answer closes the cubic k = 5 band of the growth reformulation (any cubic counterexample there would be such a graph).

## Context
