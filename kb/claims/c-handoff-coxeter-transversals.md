# Coxeter graph: dyadic cycle counts and transversal numbers

## Statement

The Coxeter graph (28 vertices) has exactly 21 distinct 8-cycles and 609 distinct 16-cycles; the minimum vertex transversal of its 8-cycles has size 6 and that of its 16-cycles has size 5.
