# EG ⟺ f(k) ≥ 2^(k+1) for all k

## Statement

Let f(k) be the minimum order of a graph with δ ≥ 3 and no cycle of length 2^2,…,2^k. Then the Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture holds iff f(k) ≥ 2^(k+1) for every k ≥ 2.

## Evidence

- `kb/runs/2026-11-16-dossier/program/STRATEGY_2026-11-17.md#2`

## Caveats

Elementary and likely folklore. Known constructions give f(4) ≤ 82, f(5) ≤ 370, f(6) ≤ 26,550 (unrefereed sources), far above the thresholds 32, 64, 128.
