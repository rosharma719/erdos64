# 2-separated pockets and every edge give lengths differing by 1 or 2 (from Fan 2002)

## Statement

If δ(G) ≥ 3 and (A,B) is a separation with A∩B = {u,v} and (G[B],u,v) rooted 2-connected, then any cycle through B can be rerouted inside B to change its length by 1 or 2. In a 2-connected δ≥3 graph every edge lies on two cycles whose lengths differ by 1 or 2.
