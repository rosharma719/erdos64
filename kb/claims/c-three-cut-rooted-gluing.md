# Rooted robustness glues across nontrivial 3-edge cuts

## Statement

If G is 3-connected cubic with a nontrivial 3-edge cut (A,B), and the contractions G_A, G_B are root-robust, then G is root-robust. A minimum counterexample to root-robustness (n ≥ 10) has, on the side of every nontrivial 3-cut away from the root, a triangle or one of two specific 7-vertex pieces.

## Evidence

- `kb/runs/2026-11-16-dossier/program/STRATEGY_2026-11-17.md#3`

## Caveats

The exceptions (c-rooted-robustness-fails-n16) show the triangle/7-piece sides are genuine obstructions; rooted gluing cannot by itself reduce EG to prime cubic graphs.
