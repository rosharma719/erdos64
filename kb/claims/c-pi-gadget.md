# Π-gadget: a position- and modulus-free length adjuster

## Statement

If a path Π outside a path M meets M through rungs at x, y, w (same order along both, middle rung a single edge), then among the three reroutings of M through Π some length change is ≢ 0 (mod m) for every m ≥ 3; in general s1 + s2 − s3 = 2·t_y. Five rungs always contain three in matching order (Erdős–Szekeres).

## Caveats

Elementary; appears new. Requires robust embedding of the middle rung in practice (see q-robust-linkage).
