# EG for 2-connected bounded-degree δ≥3 expanders (conditional)

## Statement

Every sufficiently large 2-connected α-expander with 3 ≤ δ ≤ Δ(G) ≤ Δ contains cycles of every even length in a linear range, in particular a cycle whose length is a power of two; and cycles of all residues mod every odd k (answering the bounded-degree case of Martinsson–Steiner's question).
