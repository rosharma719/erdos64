# Induced cycles are ±1/±2 length adjusters when δ ≥ 3

## Statement

If δ(G) ≥ 3 and Q is an induced cycle, any two vertices of Q have edges leaving Q; entering and leaving Q at two vertices at arc distance ⌊(q−1)/2⌋ gives two routes whose lengths differ by 1 or 2.

## Evidence

- `kb/runs/2026-11-16-dossier/program/STRATEGY_2026-11-17.md#4`

## Caveats

Elementary. Attachment (reaching those two vertices disjointly) is the real difficulty; see later claims.
