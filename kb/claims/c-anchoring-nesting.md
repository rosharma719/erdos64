# Few rigid candidates: anchoring and nesting lemmas

## Statement

In a 2-connected bounded-degree α-expander with δ ≥ 3, after removing a set W′ with robust core U, every rigid (mod p) candidate cycle is anchored at a defect, and each defect anchors at most κ/β + 1 pairwise-disjoint candidates. Hence at most O(Δ|W′|κ/β) of any family of disjoint candidates are rigid, for every α.
