# Short cycles of nonzero residue in 2-connected δ≥3 expanders, for every α

## Statement

For every α ∈ (0,1] there is C(α) such that every 2-connected α-expander on n ≥ n0 vertices with δ ≥ 3 contains, for every odd prime p, a cycle of length ≢ 0 (mod p) and at most C log n.

## Evidence

- `kb/runs/2026-11-16-dossier/passes/17_short_nonzero_residue.md#1`

## Caveats

Full proof written; needs independent refereeing. Appears new: Martinsson–Steiner obtain all residues only for α > 1/(p−1); Fan gives a nonzero residue without a length bound. The mod-4 analogue is not covered.
