# Rooted robustness fails for 3-connected cubic graphs (K4, two on 8, one on 16 vertices)

## Statement

Among all 3-connected cubic graphs on ≤ 16 vertices (generated by edge-bridging from K4; class counts 1, 2, 4, 14, 57, 341, 2828 match OEIS A204198), exactly K4, two 8-vertex graphs (K4 with two vertices truncated; K3,3 with one vertex truncated) and one 16-vertex graph (graph6 `OJO?_U?_kA?@H?B?___@H`) have a vertex lying on every dyadic cycle.

## Caveats

The 16-vertex graph is not cyclically 4-edge-connected, so the dossier's rooted *prime* conjecture is unaffected. The n=16 instance was confirmed by exhaustive NetworkX cycle enumeration.
