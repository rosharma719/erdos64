# Cubic graphs with every edge on a C3 or C5 contain C4 or C8

## Statement

If a cubic graph has every edge on a triangle or a pentagon, it contains a C4 or a C8. Equivalently, a cubic {C4,C8}-free graph has an edge lying on neither a C3 nor a C5.

## Evidence

- `kb/runs/2026-11-17-handoff/handoff/Erdos_Gyarfas_Full_Research_Dossier.md#4.2`
- `kb/runs/2026-11-17-handoff/packets/EG_Proof_Route_Short_Cycle_Theorem/RESEARCH_NOTE.md`

## Caveats

The proof (pentagon overlap lemma plus the triangle case via a seven-vertex three-port gadget) is in an archived note. It has not been refereed. It is specific to cubic graphs; a 165-vertex 4-regular example (Run6) shows the higher-degree analogue fails.
