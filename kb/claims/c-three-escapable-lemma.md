# Three escape points on a cycle of length ≢ 0 mod m give a step ≢ 0 mod m

## Statement

For a cycle of length q and any three of its vertices, if m is an odd prime power (or a power of 2) not dividing q, some pair has arc-length difference ≢ 0 mod m. Moreover a cycle of length ≥ 2k/β + k in a β-expander has k vertices with vertex-disjoint routes to any set of more than half the vertices plus k.
